Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import a recipe from Instagram or TikTok?

Open the post in the Instagram or TikTok app, tap the Share button, and choose DinnerSorted from the share sheet. The app will extract the recipe automatically. You'll be taken to a review screen where you can confirm or edit the details before saving.

How do I import a recipe from a website?

In the app, tap the + button and choose "Import from URL". Paste the recipe URL and tap Import. You can also share a page directly from Safari using the share sheet.

My recipe import didn't work — what should I do?

A few things can cause this:

  • The post or page may not contain enough recipe information for the AI to extract.
  • Some accounts have private posts that can't be read by the share extension.
  • Your internet connection may have dropped during the import.

Try again with a stable connection. If it keeps failing, you can always add the recipe manually using the "Enter manually" option.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the app, go to Account, and tap "Manage Subscription". If you've previously subscribed with the same Apple ID, your Pro access will be restored. You can also tap "Restore purchases" on the paywall screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select DinnerSorted. You can cancel there at any time. If you cancel during your free trial, you won't be charged.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account → Delete Account in the app. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.
